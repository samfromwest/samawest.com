About

I studied theatre at Dartmouth, where I was a Mellon Mays Undergraduate Fellow. My senior honors thesis was a play I wrote and produced as a workshop reading. I also wrote for Black Praxis, covering African American student life and culture on campus.

After school I worked at Manhattan Theatre Club as assistant to Executive Producer Barry Grove, where I ran communications with the board and senior leadership, managed the awards-season budget, and helped lead the company's diversity, equity, and inclusion work.

These days I'm an Associate Producer of Audio at Simon & Schuster, producing and directing audiobooks, and a podcast producer at Mentor A Promise. Freelance, I produce podcasts and design sound for theatre.

I also write, mostly poetry, on my Substack.

What ties it together: I like building the conditions for people to tell a story well, whether that's a recording schedule, a mix, or a room where a nervous guest feels at ease.
